The idea behind Causative Actions & Self Control
Age 18

Into the system

E'drick enters the California prison system as a young man — the same age many of the youth he now serves are approaching.

20+ years

Learning the cost

More than two decades inside reveal the pattern: decisions made in seconds, consequences that last decades.

The work

Writing the curriculum

He builds Causative Actions and Self Control — programs that let youth experience consequences in a classroom before they ever meet them in real life.

CDCR

Proven inside the walls

The Causative Actions Program has been implemented within the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation.

Today

Bridging Sacramento

Youth diversion tours, healing circles and mentorship connect young people to a different road — and to the work and opportunity at the end of it.

Programs

See it. Feel it. Choose different.

Every program is built from lived experience and designed for young people, families, schools and agencies who want prevention that actually lands.
