We build the bridge before they need a way back

The Public Bridge
Every young person has potential

The difference is whether someone reaches them early enough, believes in them strongly enough, and connects them to opportunities that expand what they believe is possible.

The Public Bridge unites youth, families, schools, mentors, community leaders, higher education, public agencies and credible lived experience. Through education, mentorship, accountability, family engagement, leadership development and real-world exposure, young people learn that every decision has a destination, and their future is worth protecting.

Our missionBridging social and economic gaps of disparity through community unification, peer mentorship and preventative support programs.
